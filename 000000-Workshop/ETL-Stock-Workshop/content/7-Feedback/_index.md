#### Feedback

#### Overall Evaluation

##### 1. Working Environment
The working environment throughout the First Cloud AI Journey program is very friendly, professional, and open. FCAJ mentors and members are always willing to support whenever I encounter technical challenges or AWS architecture roadblocks, even outside regular hours. The workspace and technical infrastructure are well-structured, helping me focus on building the Big Data ETL pipeline and ML models effectively.

##### 2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin
The mentors provided very detailed and enthusiastic guidance, actively supporting the team in clearly explaining complex AWS concepts and architecture optimization assessments. Mentors always encouraged the team to think for themselves and solve problems independently, rather than simply providing direct answers. The admin team assisted with administrative procedures, cloud resources, and created the most favorable working environment for the team.

##### 3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major
The tasks I learned, and subsequently applied to the project-building an automated ETL pipeline, calculating time series features using Polars, training the XGBoost model, and deploying a REST API on API Gateway, are entirely aligned with my knowledge of Data Science and Information Technology. The project helped the team connect university theory with enterprise Cloud Engineering & MLOps practices.

##### 4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities
During the 8-week program, I acquired crucial cloud and engineering skills: mastering AWS Lambda, Amazon S3 data layering, SQS queuing, Docker containerization, and high-performance data processing with Polars and Parquet. Beyond technical skills, I enhanced project management, technical documentation, and problem-solving methodologies in a real-world cloud environment.

##### 5. Company Culture & Team Spirit
The FCAJ culture is highly positive and collaborative: everyone respects individual contributions, approaches technical tasks with rigor, and maintains an engaging environment. When facing complex deployment challenges, team members collaborate seamlessly to resolve bottlenecks. This strong team spirit made me feel like an integral part of the cloud engineering team.

##### 6. Internship Policies / Benefits
The program provides great learning benefits, access to AWS cloud credits/resources, structured milestone reviews, and opportunities to attend technical sharing sessions. Access to real-world cloud architecture practice is a significant highlight.

#### Additional Questions

* **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**
  * I had the opportunity to learn a great deal about cloud computing, and I successfully applied my knowledge to build a complete automated Serverless ETL & ML system from scratch, processing over 3,000 stock tickers within the AWS Free Tier and seeing raw data transform into real-time predictive results on the dashboard. The program had truly beneficial events; I learned a lot through the meetups and hope the program will invite many more high-quality speakers to share their experiences with participating students.
* **What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**
  * Organize more sessions and record hands-on video tutorials on cloud tools to help create Cloud infrastructure more automatically. Create more conditions for students participating in the program to go to the office.
* **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**
  * I've recommended the program to some friends who want to learn more about cloud or data. The FCAJ program offers invaluable hands-on AWS Cloud experience, dedicated mentoring, and a fantastic environment for those who want to pursue a career as a Cloud & Data Engineer.

---

#### Suggestions & Expectations

* **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**
  * Regularly organize technical meetups between teams to present architectures, demo products, and receive feedback from solution architects. Strengthen networking and interaction among teams to foster a sense of unity.
* **Would you like to continue this program in the future?**
  * Yes, I would love to continue participating in advanced FCAJ phases, diving deeper into MLOps automation, real-time streaming architectures (Kinesis/Kafka), and enterprise cloud solutions.
* **Any other comments (free sharing):**
  * Sincere thanks to mentor Lữ Hoàn Thiện, the FCAJ team, and all the teammates for creating a challenging, yet exciting, inspiring, and successful journey of over two months!
